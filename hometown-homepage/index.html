Visit Borås

Enjoy the culture and architecture in this vibrant city of Sweden.

Top three activities to do in Borås

Enjoy the Architectural Landmarks of Borås

Wander through the city centre and admire the stunning mix of modern and historic architecture that makes Borås truly unique.

Explore Borås Djurpark

One of Sweden's most popular zoos, home to hundreds of animals. A perfect day out for families and animal lovers alike.

Discover the Textile Museum

Borås is known as Sweden's textile capital. Visit the Textile Museum to learn about the city's rich industrial history and creative heritage.
